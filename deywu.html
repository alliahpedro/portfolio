01

Overview

Know where the shuttle is before you leave for the stop.

Deywu is a mobile app concept for the Asia Pacific College campus shuttle. Students see the shuttle on a live map, check arrival estimates and upcoming stops, and plan their pickup before they head out for class.

The concept also covers CCTV views of the waiting shed and the bus interior, simple seat booking and a built-in feedback form for reporting service issues.

02

Problem

Students were guessing about their ride.

01

Where is it?

Students often don't know where the shuttle is.

02

When will it arrive?

There is no clear arrival time to plan around.

03

Are there seats left?

Students can't tell whether the shuttle has room before it arrives.

That means long waits at the stop, missed rides, and no easy way to report service issues.

03

Design Decisions

How the app answers each question.

01

A Live Map with Arrival Estimates

The shuttle's position, its route and the upcoming stops sit on one map, with an arrival time in minutes, so "where" and "when" are answered at a glance.

02

CCTV of the Shed and the Bus

Students can look at the waiting shed and the bus interior before they leave, which helps answer whether there is room.

03

Simple Seat Booking and Feedback

Booking a seat takes few steps, and a built-in feedback form gives students a direct way to report service issues.

04

Bottom Sheets and Three Tabs

Details open in bottom sheets that keep the map visible, and a three-tab navigation bar keeps the app easy to learn. The look follows APC's blue and yellow.

Solution Showcase

Two views of the app.

01Live Map

Overview

The home screen of the app. It opens on a map headed "Know Where the Shuttle Is" so the main question is answered first.

Key Highlights

  • A route line from the shuttle to Asia Pacific College, with the shuttle marked on the map.
  • A Morning Schedule bottom sheet listing the next departure, here 7 mins to Humabon (Magallanes Ave.), in a yellow strip.
  • The sheet can expand, and the map stays visible behind it.
  • A three-icon tab bar at the bottom with bus, camera and person icons.

Outcome

Students can see the shuttle, the next stop and the wait time without leaving the map.

02Onboarding

Overview

Four screens, one idea each, before the student reaches the live map.

Key Highlights

  • Live Shuttle Tracking: see the shuttle's live location and estimated arrival time at a glance.
  • Plan Your Pickup: check routes and upcoming stops before heading out for class.
  • Follow Every Trip: track the shuttle in real time and get notified about service updates.
  • Ride With Confidence: start tracking and reach the stop right on time.

Outcome

The photos of the real APC shuttle and its interior set the context early, and each screen ends in one clear button: Next, then Start Tracking.
