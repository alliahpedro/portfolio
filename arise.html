01

Overview

One account for everything a member owes and everything they can join.

The ARISE PH Member Portal is a web app for the partners of ARISE Philippines, the Private Sector Alliance for Disaster Resilient Societies. Members sign in to submit reports, upload knowledge products, complete their membership details and keep up with events and announcements.

The member home brings these together in one view, with a sidebar for the rest of the portal: Submit Report, Membership, Knowledge Products, Skills Builds, Reports and a System Guide Manual.

02

Problem

Compliance lived in email threads and long forms.

Partners managed compliance through scattered email threads and multi-page forms with no progress tracking, which led to lost documents and missed deadlines.

03

Goals

What the portal had to fix.

01

Keep Documents in One Place

Replace email attachments with a single place to submit reports and upload knowledge products.

02

Show Progress

Give every report and activity a visible status, so members and staff can see what is done, pending or in review.

03

Prevent Missed Deadlines

Put due dates and announcements where members see them as soon as they sign in.

04

Shorten the Forms

Collect company details and proof of registration in one clear form instead of several pages.

04

Design Decisions

How the problem shaped the screens.

01

Actions First on the Home Page

Submit Report and Upload Knowledge Product sit at the top of the member home as two large buttons, ahead of events and activity, because they are the two things members come to do.

02

Deadlines Beside Events

Upcoming Events and Important Announcements share one row, and announcements carry due dates such as the Q4 accomplishment report, so a deadline is hard to miss.

03

Status on Every Item

Recent Reports mark each item as submitted or due, and the activity feed uses chips such as Done, Pending, Review and Confirmed with a matching action beside each one.

04

Drag-and-Drop Uploads

Each report type has its own upload area that accepts PDF, JPG, PNG, SVG and ZIP files, so members drop the file where it belongs and see which types are allowed.

05

Solution Showcase

Three screens from the portal.

01Member Home

Overview

The Member's Account page is the landing screen after sign-in. It shows what is happening today and what needs attention.

Key Highlights

  • Four summary tiles: reports submitted, attended events, active members and knowledge items.
  • Submit Report in green and Upload Knowledge Product in white, side by side.
  • Upcoming Events with dates, times and venues, next to Important Announcements with due dates.
  • Recent Activity, Member's Recent Activity and Recent Reports for history at a glance.

Outcome

Members see their next task, their next deadline and their report history without opening another page.

02Membership Form

Overview

Company details and proof of legal registration, gathered in one form.

Key Highlights

  • Four icons across the top mark the groups of the membership process.
  • Country of the applicant company and country of legal registration sit side by side.
  • A short explanation field with a 0/100 counter for cases with no attachments.
  • Company Sector as a dropdown and a Choose File control for the proof of legal registration.

Outcome

The multi-page form becomes one screen with labelled fields and a clear place for the proof document.

03Reports and Activity

Overview

Recent member activity beside the accomplishment-report upload.

Key Highlights

  • Four upload areas: Quarterly Accomplishment Report, Good Practices, Accomplishment Reports and Success Stories.
  • Accepted file types are printed under each area.
  • Cancel and Update buttons close the form, with Update as the filled primary action.
  • The activity feed pairs each entry with a status chip and a View, Review, Download, Preview or Manage action.

Outcome

Staff and members can see what was uploaded, what is pending and what needs review in the same place.
